Artist:  futureazoo
Source:  TextureRepository, https://github.com/Futureazoo/TextureRepository
         (folder: futureazoo/)

Colours and style in this pack were taken from futureazoo's work:
  - Iron ramp (353535 to e5e5e5), sampled from futureazoo's iron machine set.
  - Durasteel ramp (525660 to e9eaec), sampled from the durasteel set.
  - Brass ramp (6b4e00 to f3d174), sampled from the bronze machine set.
  - The plate style - dark 1px outline, top-left highlight, brushed rows,
    corner rivets - follows futureazoo's machine plates.

Used in every texture in this pack except block/materials/plastic, block/materials/stone,
block/materials/stone_brick, block/materials/wood, the infinite sources and the
items: the machine panels,
item filters, fluid drain, discharge, tank, pipe and valve, conveyor, steel
frame, TechReborn converter, and the steel, steel_dark, iron, invar, brass,
copper and metal_panel materials.
